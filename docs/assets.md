# Asset provenance

The four playback control PNGs in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi` come directly from the user-supplied `open_city_radio_sprites_and_screens.zip` (sprites/buttons). They are neutral geometric controls.

Station badges remain in a separate private import pack, outside the repository-ready source tree. Reference screenshots are design references only; they are not composited into interactive screens. No game soundtrack or commercial music is included.

The built-in demo WAVs are original synthetic sine-wave loops generated by `tools/make_demo_pack.py`. The fallback neon artwork is drawn by `RadioArtView` at runtime. No license is asserted for third-party marks or supplied artwork.
